## Overview

Pantheon is the self-hosted engineering infrastructure behind my development and R&D work. It is positioned as lab infrastructure rather than hobbyist homelab content: Linux systems, networking, storage, containers, observability, GPU-capable compute, and documented operations.

For Loose Arrow Labs, this matters because prototype systems increasingly need local services, model inference, device telemetry collectors, build environments, storage, and repeatable deployment workflows.

## Engineering Problem

A technical studio needs infrastructure that can support experimentation without becoming fragile:

- Services should be reproducible and documented
- Networks should separate concerns and reduce accidental coupling
- Storage should be deliberate, backed up, and understandable
- AI and automation workloads need compute and operating boundaries
- Failures should produce logs and troubleshooting paths, not guesswork

## What It Supports

- Dockerized engineering tools and internal services
- Local AI and retrieval workflow experimentation
- Storage and backup patterns for project artifacts
- Network segmentation and service isolation concepts
- Monitoring, logging, and operational runbooks
- Repeatable workstation/server setup patterns
