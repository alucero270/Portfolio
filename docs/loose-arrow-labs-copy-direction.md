## How A Project Runs

### 1. Understand The System

Define what exists, what is failing, and what matters.

- clarify constraints
- identify interfaces
- isolate unknowns

### 2. Build Or Stabilize

Implement or repair the smallest version that works.

- incremental changes
- observable outputs
- controlled iteration

### 3. Validate And Document

Make the system understandable and repeatable.

- verify behavior
- document decisions
- leave something usable and maintainable

## How I Work

I focus on systems that need to function under real constraints, not just look correct.

### Principles

**Clear boundaries**  
Define where systems start and stop.

**Explicit interfaces**  
Inputs, outputs, and expectations are visible.

**Testability**  
Systems should be observable and verifiable.

**Validation over assumption**  
Behavior is checked, not guessed.

**Documentation as part of the work**  
Systems should be explainable after they are built.

**Physical reality matters**  
Timing, power, noise, and mechanical limits are part of the design.

## Proof / Evidence

Work is documented through:

- repositories and source code
- system diagrams and architecture notes
- validation logs and test results
- screenshots and output traces
- written breakdowns of decisions and tradeoffs

The goal is not just to show what was built, but how it behaves and why it was designed that way.
